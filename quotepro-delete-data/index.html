Delete your QuotePro data

QuotePro, published by Freshinnov Labs (Private) Limited · Last updated 12 September 2026

This page explains how to delete data relating to QuotePro: Quotes & Invoices, published by Freshinnov Labs (Private) Limited. There are two separate things you may want to remove, and they work differently.

1. Your business records, which are on your phone

Your quotes, invoices, customers, prices, logo, signature and settings are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. We never receive a copy and cannot delete them for you — but you can remove all of them yourself, instantly:

  1. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy, from Settings → Export Backup.
  2. Uninstall QuotePro, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → QuotePro → Storage → Clear data.

Either removes every business record the app holds. This cannot be undone.

2. Your licence record, which we hold

We hold one small record per licensed device: an anonymous device code, the activation token used, and the dates the licence runs between. To have it deleted, ask us.

How to request deletion

  1. Email freshinnovlabs@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line Delete my QuotePro data
  3. Include your activation token, or the phone number or email address you bought the licence with, so we can find the right record

We reply within 30 days, and usually within a few working days. There is no charge.

What is deleted, and what we keep

DataWhat happensKept for
Device code (the anonymous identifier for your phone) Deleted —
Activation token and its licence dates Deleted —
Your quotes, invoices, customers and settings Never held by us; you delete them on your device —
Record of your purchase, for accounting Kept As required of any Zimbabwean business for tax and audit purposes
Google Play subscription records, if you subscribed through Play Held by Google, not by us Managed under Google's own policy

Please note: deleting your licence record ends the licence on that device. The app will return to asking for activation. If you later want to use QuotePro again you will need a new licence.

Subscriptions bought through Google Play

If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel the subscription in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future payments; deleting the data you hold with Google is handled through your Google Account.
