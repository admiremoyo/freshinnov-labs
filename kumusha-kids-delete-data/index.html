Delete your Kumusha Kids data

Kumusha Kids, by FRESHINNOV LABS (PRIVATE) LIMITED

Kumusha Kids has no accounts. The only record we keep on our server is for a phone: a device identifier, when its free trial started, and whether it has been activated or subscribed. Everything else, including a child's name if one was typed in, stays on the phone.

How to ask us to delete it

  1. Open Kumusha Kids, press and hold the grown-ups button on the home screen, and note the version line at the bottom of the For grown-ups page.
  2. Email freshinnovlabs@gmail.com with the subject "Kumusha Kids data deletion", saying which phone it is (make and model) and, if you used one, the activation code.
  3. We delete the phone's record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted: the phone's trial and activation record, and its link to any activation code it used.

Kept: nothing that identifies the phone. A used activation code stays marked as used so it cannot be used again. Google Play keeps its own record of any subscription; cancel that in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Deleting the record ends any activation on that phone. To remove everything kept on the phone itself, uninstall the app.
