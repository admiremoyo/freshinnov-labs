Goho Privacy Policy

Freshinnov Labs (Private) Limited · Last updated 26 October 2026

Goho is a farming app for Zimbabwean farmers (Android package zw.co.freshinnovlabs.goho), published by Freshinnov Labs (Private) Limited, 13965 Eastview Phase 2, Goromonzi, Zimbabwe. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain terms.

The short version: you do not need an account to use Goho, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number. Crop photos are analysed on your phone and never leave it. Your location is used only to show local weather and to map your fields, and we do not keep it.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us and we cannot see it:

  • Photos you take or choose for crop disease diagnosis
  • Your diagnosis history (crop, detected disease, advice and the photo)
  • The fields you draw on the map and their saved crop-health readings
  • Whether you have already seen the app's introduction screens

Disease detection runs on a model built into the app, so it works without an internet connection.

What leaves your device

WhatWhyWho receives it
Your device's location To show the weather forecast for where you are Our weather service on Supabase, which asks OpenWeather for the forecast. Used for that request only and not stored.
The boundary of a field you have drawn To produce satellite crop-health (NDVI) images and statistics for that field The Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (Sentinel Hub), the European satellite-imagery service. Sent only when you ask for a reading.
The map area on screen To draw the base map when you view or map fields OpenStreetMap and Esri ArcGIS map-tile servers

The app also downloads public information from us: Mbare Musika commodity prices, crop guides, planting seasons, supplier and institution listings, and their images. These are stored on servers operated by Supabase. Like any web service, those servers keep short-lived technical logs, such as the IP address and time of a request, for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify you.

There is no advertising in Goho and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone, and we never use it for advertising. The services named above process data only to provide the feature you used.

AI advice

This version of Goho does not send anything to an online AI service. If a future version offers AI-written farming advice, only the crop name, the detected disease and the confidence level would be sent to OpenAI to write that advice. Your photo and your location would not be sent. We will update this page before any such change.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location — for local weather and for mapping your fields with GPS.
  • Camera — to photograph a leaf for disease diagnosis. You can choose a photo from your gallery instead.
  • Internet — to load prices, crop guides, weather and satellite data.

You can refuse location or camera. The rest of the app keeps working; weather, field mapping or diagnosis from the camera will simply be unavailable.

How long we keep it

We do not keep your location or your field boundaries; they are used for the request and discarded. Everything else stays on your phone until you delete it.

Your choices

You can delete individual diagnoses and fields inside the app. You can delete everything Goho has stored by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Because Goho has no accounts, we hold no personal profile about you. If you have a question or a request, write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Goho is a tool for farmers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.
