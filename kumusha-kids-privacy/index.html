Kumusha Kids Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kumusha Kids is a learning app for children in ECD A and ECD B (ages 3 to 6), made in Zimbabwe by FRESHINNOV LABS (PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what we never do.

The short version

  • We do not ask for, collect or store a child's name, photo, voice, location or contacts.
  • There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics in the app.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone for marketing.
  • The only thing sent to our server is an identifier for the phone, so the free trial and a paid activation work, and cannot be reset by reinstalling.

What the app sends, and why

WhatWhyWhere it goes
A device identifier (Android ID, which is not the advertising ID), the date the trial started, and whether the phone is activated To give each phone one 3-day free trial, and to unlock the app after a subscription or an activation code Our database, hosted by Supabase
The activation code typed in, if one is used To check the code and make sure it is used only once Our database, hosted by Supabase
The app version, and whether a Google Play subscription is active So we can help a parent who contacts us, and know which version they have Our database, hosted by Supabase
Requests to download a learning video or a document To deliver the video or document Supabase storage
Purchases Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see card or payment details. Google Play

Like any internet service, our hosting provider sees the phone's IP address when the app connects. We do not use it to identify anyone.

The app also downloads its fonts from Google Fonts when first opened online.

What stays on the phone

A child's first name, if a parent types one in so the app can greet the child, is kept only on the phone. It is never sent to us. Stars, progress and settings also stay on the phone.

Children

Kumusha Kids is made for young children and is designed to collect as little as possible. Anything that leaves the app (opening a document in another app, or subscribing) is behind a grown-up check that a young child cannot pass. The app has no chat, no sharing and no links to social media.

How long we keep it

We keep a phone's trial and activation record while the app is in use, so that paid time is not lost if the app is reinstalled. A parent can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

Your choices

To ask what we hold about a phone, or to have it deleted, email us with the phone's build details from the app's For grown-ups page. Deleting the record ends any activation on that phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything kept on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
